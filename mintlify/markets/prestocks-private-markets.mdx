## Valuation Context: Implied vs. Issuer Mark

Unlike public equities with continuous exchange ticks, private companies are benchmarked against their **Issuer Mark** (the latest verified funding round or secondary tender valuation). Sisera derives and tracks:

- **Live Token Price:** Current Solana market price for the PreStocks token.
- **Issuer Mark Price & Valuation:** The reference share price and total enterprise valuation from the company's latest verified private mark.
- **Implied Valuation:** The market capitalization implied by the live Solana token price relative to the issuer mark.
- **Gap to Mark (`Premium / Discount %`):**
  $$\text{Gap to Mark (\%)} = \frac{\text{Implied Valuation} - \text{Issuer Mark Valuation}}{\text{Issuer Mark Valuation}} \times 100$$

## Multi-Factor Private Market Rankings

Through `GET /v1/rankings/private`, operators can rank and filter all PreStocks markets across seven institutional dimensions:

| Ranking Mode | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `gap_to_mark` | Ranks private markets by absolute dislocation from their verified issuer valuation mark. |
| `deepest_discount` | Surfaces pre-IPO tokens trading at the largest discount relative to their latest private round. |
| `highest_premium` | Surfaces pre-IPO tokens commanding the highest secondary market premium. |
| `liquidity` | Ranks by executable onchain pool liquidity and lowest price impact. |
| `momentum` | Ranks by multi-timeframe price and volume acceleration. |
| `news_intensity` | Ranks by the volume and severity of corroborated fundraising, M&A, executive, or regulatory events. |
| `portfolio_fit` | Scores each PreStocks market against your **current portfolio**, penalizing sector over-concentration and rewarding diversification. |

## Executing & Automating PreStocks

You can trade any PreStocks market directly from the ranking table or detail view:

1. **Direct Swap Execution:** Route paper or live Solana orders via Jupiter (`POST /v1/solana/orders/prepare` and `POST /v1/solana/orders/:id/execute`) with pre-trade risk verification.
2. **Conditional Policy Automation:** Ask Sisera AI to compile a valuation-gated policy, for example:
   > *"Buy $2,000 of OpenAI if the premium to mark falls below 3%, pool liquidity stays above $50,000, and no high-severity negative regulatory event is active."*
